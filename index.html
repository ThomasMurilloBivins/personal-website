About Me

I'm a cybersecurity student at the University of North Georgia enrolled in the Honors & Cyber Leadership Development Programs. My work spans both the offensive and defensive spectrum — from competing in national CTF competitions to publishing research on adversarial AI capabilities.

My research focuses on agentic AI systems and their potential to amplify cyber attacks. I've presented this work at two academic symposia, exploring how autonomous AI agents change the threat landscape and what defenders need to do about it.

Outside of academics I've gained hands-on experience through consulting internships and am always working toward my next certification or CTF podium.
